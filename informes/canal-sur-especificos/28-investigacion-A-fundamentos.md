## Tema 1 · Fundamentos de sonido (lo que falta: fase; apoyo para amplitud y dinámica)

El RTVE 02/04 y edicion-montaje/03 cubren onda, frecuencia, periodo, longitud de onda, amplitud, decibelio,
ruido rosa/blanco, isofónicas, margen audible, enmascaramiento, timbre y acústica de salas. La fase sólo aparece
de pasada (pines 2/3 «en fase/contrafase»; «desfase de los envolventes»). Material para un epígrafe breve:

### 1.1 Fase

- Definición operativa. DPA-PPD: «Phase can only be expressed if the actual waveform is compared to a reference or
  another waveform – and at a given frequency. The phase shift occurs due to a shift in time, a delay. The time
  shift may stem from two microphones' different distances to a sound source. Or due to the application of a delay
  line. Or due to the physics of a given electrical filter. Usually, we describe a phase shift by the so-called
  phase angle in the range of ±180°».
- Fase ↔ tiempo (depende de la frecuencia). DPA-PPD: «The duration of one period of 1 kHz is 1 ms (1/1000 s). A
  phase shift of 90° (a quarter of a period) equals a time shift of 0.25 ms. The duration of one period of two kHz
  is 0.5 ms … A phase shift of 90° … equals a time shift of 0.125 ms. As shown, the time shift of a constant phase
  shift varies with frequency».
- El oído y la fase. DPA-PPD: «the resulting waveforms are very different even though containing exactly the same
  frequency components. (However, they sound the same if reproduced in a linear system, as the ear only to a minor
  degree is sensitive to phase)».
- Filtros y fase (enlaza con tema 5). DPA-PPD: «Most equalizers or equalizing circuits may exhibit frequency-dependent
  phase shifts».

### 1.2 Polaridad no es fase

- DPA-PPD: «Changing the polarity is a simple swap of the negative part becoming positive and the positive part then
  becoming negative. The signal is inverted … the signal is multiplied by "-1"»; «Sometimes, it is said that the
  phase is shifted by 180°. This is, however, not exactly correct. A phase shift requires a time shift, i.e., a
  delay, which is not involved in swopping polarity. On the other hand, after inversion, the signal is now 180° out
  of phase».
- El botón. DPA-PPD: «The Greek letter φ [small letter phi] on the button indicates the function of inversion.
  Sometimes, it looks like the letter Ø».
- Convenio de polaridad del micrófono. DPA-PPD: «in the case of balanced XLR connections, it is decided that pin 2
  always represents the in-phase signal of the acting sound pressure. So, rising pressure on the diaphragm, moving
  the diaphragm inward, provides a rising positive voltage on pin 2. This is true for balanced condenser microphones
  for professional audio …, while dynamic microphones behave a little differently».
- Lóbulo trasero invertido (apoyo al tema 3). DPA-PPD: «Directional microphones, like supercardioids, hypercardioids,
  or figures-of-eights … pick up frontal sound and, by various amounts, sound from the rear. However, the polarity of
  the sound picked from the rear is inverted». (Coincide con RTVE 05: bidireccional «con polaridad» opuesta.)

### 1.3 Interferencia y filtro en peine

- DPA-COMB: «Comb filtering occurs when a sound adds to itself within a short time interval. This interval typically
  ranges from less than one ms to approximately 25 ms. Comb filtering can emerge in two ways: Due to reflections;
  Because more than one microphone is open and picking up the same signal at different positions. In addition, in
  order for comb filtering to occur, the levels of the signals must be within 10 dB from each other».
- Nombre. DPA-COMB: «the effect acts as a filter with a frequency response looking like a comb for the hair».
- Caso de plató (útil para tertulias/informativos). DPA-COMB: «In a talk studio, sometimes you can hear that the
  host's microphone may sound a little muddy, while the interviewed guest's microphone sounds much cleaner … the
  voice of the host is picked up by two microphones with some distance between them».
- Remedios. DPA-COMB: «Attenuate delayed sound picked up by the microphone by at least 10 dB to minimize the problem
  of comb filtering»; regla 3:1: «a neighboring microphone should be at least three times further away, (given the
  sensitivity and the gain is the same on both microphones). (20 *log (1/3) ≈ -10 dB)»; en línea equidistante
  «should be 4.5:1»; reflexiones: quitar o girar el reflector, absorber, o colocar el micro sobre la superficie:
  «In this way, it becomes a boundary layer microphone».
- Medida de fase en emisión: ver tema 13, 13.7 (correlador −1/0/+1, RTW).

### 1.4 Dinámica (apoyo)

- Rango dinámico: RN155 «The dynamic range of an audio passage is the ratio of the loudest signal to the quietest
  signal» y la cifra de fabricante «120 dB dynamic range of normal human hearing» (ver 5.1). Para la audición, el
  RTVE 02 §7 (margen audible) ya da los umbrales; no se ha buscado otra fuente.

## Tema 2 · Electricidad y electrónica aplicada (lo que falta: masa, bucles y ruido)

El RTVE 01/11 da la masa sólo como «pin 1: malla o masa» y el zumbido de masa de pasada. Material Rane:

### 2.1 El bucle de masa

- Mecanismo. RN110: «Ground loops occur when the grounds of the two units are also tied together in another
  place: via the third wire in the line cord, by tying the metal chassis together through the rack rails, etc.
  These situations create a circuit through which current may flow in a closed "loop" from one unit's ground out
  to a second unit and back to the first. It is not simply the presence of this current that creates the hum --
  it is when this current flows through a unit's audio signal ground that creates the hum».
- Frecuencia: RTVE 01 ya asocia «50 Hz europeo» a problemas de alimentación o masas (línea 217); no se ha buscado
  aquí otra fuente.
- Por qué el balanceado lo aguanta. RN110: «The mere presence of this ground loop current is no cause for alarm if
  your system uses properly implemented and completely balanced interconnects, which are excellent at rejecting
  ground loop and other noise currents. Balanced interconnect was developed to be immune to these noise currents,
  which can never be entirely eliminated». «Balanced interconnect is not compatible with unbalanced».
- Causa casi universal del ruido. RN110: «Almost all cases of noise can be traced directly to ground loops,
  grounding or lack thereof».

### 2.2 Pin 1, malla y masa de chasis

- Norma. RN110: la AES «has issued a standards document for interconnection of pro audio equipment. It is AES48,
  titled "AES48-2005: AES standard on interconnections -- Grounding and EMC practices -- Shields of connectors in
  audio equipment containing active circuitry."» (AES48 no leída; vigencia no comprobada.)
- Regla. RN110: «Since standard XLR cables come with their shields tied to pin 1 at each end …, this means
  equipment using 3-pin, XLR-type connectors must tie pin 1 to the chassis (usually called chassis ground) -- not
  the audio signal ground as is most common».
- El «problema del pin 1». RN151: «Many audio manufacturers … connect balanced shields to audio signal ground;
  pin 1 for 3-pin (XLR-type) connectors, the sleeve on 1/4" (6.35mm) jacks. Any currents induced into the shield
  modulate the ground where the shield is terminated. This also modulates the signal referenced to that ground».
  Y: «Now that the Audio Engineering Society has adopted the "pin 2 is hot" standard …» (confirma el «pin 2
  vivo» del RTVE 11).

### 2.3 Remedios y seguridad

- Seguridad primero. RN110: «Safety regulations require that all original grounding means provided from the
  factory be left intact for safe operation». (Dato para PRL: nunca quitar la toma de tierra del enchufe para
  matar un zumbido; la frase exacta es de Rane.)
- Levantamiento de masa (ground lift). RN110: «Many units come equipped with ground lift switches. In only a few
  cases can it be shown that a ground lift switch improves ground related noise»; «Ground lifts are simply another
  Band-Aid to try in case of grounding problems»; «an entire system of properly grounded equipment, without ground
  lift switches, is guaranteed (yes guaranteed) to be hum free».
- Transformadores de aislamiento. RN151: «Transformer isolation and other interface solutions are the best
  solutions for balanced/unbalanced interconnections, though they are too costly for many systems. Even fully
  balanced systems can require isolation transformers». RN110 (sistemas mixtos): «use isolation transformers or,
  if you can't do that, try the special cable assemblies described here».
- Malla levantada y RF. RN151: «Many successfully and consistently reduce RF interference by providing an RF path
  through a small capacitor connected from the lifted end of the shield to the chassis».
- Equipos con fuente externa. RN110: «Balanced units with outboard power supplies (wall warts …) do not ground
  the chassis through the line cord. Make sure such units are solidly grounded by tying the chassis to an earth
  ground».

### 2.4 Alimentación phantom: la norma y sus cifras (el RTVE 11 da «48 V —también 24 y 12—» sin norma)

- Norma. DPA-PH: «The standard IEC 61938:2018 outlines the technical specifications. These specs concern the
  voltage, the current draw, the impedance, etc.»; «The first standard was DIN 45 596 (now superseded by the IEC
  61938:2018)». SCH: «The technical details are regulated in the IEC 61938 standard». (IEC 61938 no leída: es de
  pago. Las cifras van con la fuente del fabricante.)
- Tensión y pines. DPA-PH: «Phantom power is the standard method for powering professional condenser microphones
  via the XLR-3 connector and balanced cables. The XLR-connector's pin 2 and pin 3 both carry +48 volts ±4 volts
  DC. Pin 1 is 0 volt»; «In short, the name is P48». SCH: «48 V is used as the supply voltage, with the negative
  pole to ground»; «The voltage is fed via two resistors, whose value is standardized and must be identical within
  each channel».
- Corriente. DPA-PH: «The IEC standard specifies that the rated available current of P48 is 7 mA, and 10 mA is the
  maximum».
- Impedancia de entrada con phantom. DPA-PH: «with phantom-powered input, … the input impedance is 3.4 kΩ».
- Por qué «fantasma». DPA-PH: «The name "phantom power" refers to the "invisibility" when connecting balanced
  microphones that do not need external power, like dynamic (moving coil) microphones».
- Tensiones menores. DPA-PH: «It has previously been an option for designers to choose between 12 volts or 24
  volts. So, there are systems out there providing less than 48 volts»; con tensión baja «the microphone's
  performance is affected, reducing the max SPL handling and increasing distortion». SCH: P12 «12 Volts, generally
  at somewhat higher current».
- Riesgos. DPA-PH: «If an unbalanced and sensitive microphone – like a ribbon microphone – is connected, it may get
  damaged. So never connect unbalanced microphones or cables to phantom-powered inputs». SCH: «Phantom powering was
  designed to be safe for microphones that weren't designed to use it, such as dynamic microphones. However, in
  unfavorable cases, e.g. faulty wiring, … in particular, plugging or unplugging it while the powering is on»; «it
  is good general practice to connect all microphones with the power supply turned off».
- Historia. DPA-PH: «Phantom power was invented in 1966 by Neumann in connection with a special delivery to NRK
  (Norwegian Broadcasting)».
- NO confirmado en fuente primaria: el valor de 6,81 kΩ de las resistencias y los 44-52 V como «rango» (sólo
  webs de terceros); se deduce de «±4 V» pero no se da como cita.

## Tema 3 · Microfonía (lo que falta: tipos de transductor y sensibilidad)

El RTVE 05 cubre diagramas polares, proximidad, elección, catálogo, parejas estéreo, ambisónico y soportes; no
explica los principios de transducción ni la sensibilidad (sólo la usa en «misma sensibilidad» del bidireccional).

### 3.1 Tipos: el transductor no es la directividad

- DPA-CD: «The difference between dynamic microphones and condensers is all about two different transducer
  principles. It has nothing to do with the directional characteristics of the microphones»; «Both dynamic and
  condenser microphones have a membrane that vibrates in accordance with the air movement around it … The
  microphone must then transform this membrane movement from acoustical energy into electrical energy».
- Alimentación. DPA-CD: «The vast majority of dynamic microphones can manage without power but there are some
  exceptions. Basically, all condenser microphones need some kind of power supply. This is primarily to supply the
  impedance converter and for non-electret condensers to charge the electrodes (membrane and back electrode).
  Active dynamic microphones need a power supply as well». DPA-PH: «Only microphones with built-in electronics need
  power. That is primarily condenser microphones. However, specific dynamic microphones (ribbon mics) with
  electronics also apply phantom power».
- Construcción del condensador. DPA-SPEC: «If the transducer is a condenser type, the diaphragm is positioned in
  front of the backplate. The space between the two is in the range of 20-50 µm»; «a condenser microphone needs an
  electronic interstage that converts the high impedance of the transducer into a relatively low impedance to feed
  longer cable-runs».
- Tópicos desmentidos (fabricante de condensadores: leer con esa cautela). DPA-CD: «Dynamic microphones are more
  robust than condensers (not necessarily true)»; «Condenser microphones are louder than dynamic microphones (not
  true) … it is just a question of sensitivity. In general, condenser microphones exhibit a higher sensitivity than
  dynamic microphones»; «Dynamic microphones can handle higher sound pressure levels than condenser microphones (not
  true)».
- Carga. DPA-CD: «Rule to remember: the microphone should be terminated with a load that is at least five to 10
  times higher than the output impedance of the microphone»; en dinámicos pasivos con carga baja se reduce «the low
  frequency output». Mismo criterio en DPA-PH: «the load (input impedance) should be 5-10 times higher than the
  source impedance».
- Cinta (ribbon): sólo consta en DPA-PH que es «dynamic» y sensible al phantom mal cableado; su principio físico no
  se ha leído en fuente primaria. Hueco declarado.

### 3.2 Sensibilidad, ruido propio, SPL máximo y rango dinámico

- Sensibilidad. DPA-SPEC (remite a «IEC 60268-4 … (2018) clause 11.2.1 Free-field sensitivity»): «Sensitivity is
  the microphone's ability to convert sound pressure into an electrical voltage»; «The free-field sensitivity
  states the voltage a microphone generates when placed in a free sound field at a sound pressure of 1 Pascal
  (which is the same as a sound pressure level (SPL) of 94 dB)»; «The free-field sensitivity is measured on-axis»;
  «The sensitivity is stated like this: xx mV per Pascal @ 1 kHz or yy dBV/Pascal @ 1 kHz. This is just two ways
  of saying the same thing». Ejemplo: «10 mV/Pa; -40 dB re. 1 V/Pa».
- Qué implica. DPA-SPEC: «A microphone with high sensitivity produces a high-voltage output and thus requires less
  amplification (gain) than a microphone with lower sensitivity. In applications with low sound pressure levels, a
  microphone with high sensitivity is required to keep the amplification noise low. In applications with extremely
  high SPL, a low-sensitivity microphone is appropriate».
- Tolerancia. DPA-SPEC: «Stated sensitivity is nominal … DPA, in general, specifies the sensitivity within ±2 dB or
  ±3 dB».
- Ruido propio (nivel de ruido equivalente). DPA-SPEC (IEC 60268-4 cláusula 16): «The equivalent noise level (also
  known as the microphone's self-noise) indicates the SPL at which the microphone produces the same output magnitude
  as its electrical self-noise»; dos medidas: «The A-weighted RMS-measure … A good result (extremely low noise) on
  this scale is usually below 15 dB(A). The ITU-R BS.468-4 standard uses a different weighting and quasi-peak
  detection, so on this scale, a good result is below 25-30 dB»; «Typically, a larger diaphragm will lead to lower
  self-noise»; «The self-noise also dictates the lower limit in the microphone's dynamic range».
- SPL máximo. DPA-SPEC: «Specifies the max SPL (RMS and peak) below which the total harmonic distortion is less than
  1%». «At DPA, we specify the SPL up to THD <1%» (criterio del fabricante; otros usan otro porcentaje: no
  comprobado).
- Rango dinámico del micrófono. DPA-SPEC: «Dynamic range is the difference between the RMS-level at which a THD of
  1% occur, and the noise floor (self-noise of the microphone, RMS, A-weighted)».
- Ejemplo de cuidado con el preamplificador. DPA-CD: «An extremely loud singer can produce a sound pressure peak
  level in the range of 150 dB, measured at the lips. If you have two microphones with a sensitivity of 1 mV and 10
  mV respectively …, you have outputs of 0.63 and 6.3 volt peak! Signals of this magnitude should instead be handled
  by the line input or the signal should be attenuated».
- Respuesta fuera de eje / campo difuso (apoyo a patrones polares). DPA-SPEC: «Even though the off-axis response of
  directional microphones indicates reduced output, it is crucial that these curves also exhibit a smooth frequency
  response. Otherwise, off-axis coloration (curtain effect) is introduced»; «The diffuse-field response curve
  illustrates how an omnidirectional microphone responds in a highly reverberant sound field».

## Tema 5 · Procesamiento de audio (lo que falta: puertas; apoyo para expansor y dinámica)

El RTVE 07 da la puerta y el expansor en dos filas de tabla («Cierra el canal cuando la señal baja del
umbral»; «Como la puerta, pero progresivo»). El enunciado pide «puertas». Material de fabricante (Rane):

### 5.1 Qué es una puerta y en qué se diferencia del expansor

- Estructura común. RN155 cap. 1: «All dynamics processors have the common structure …: a gain control element
  in the main signal path and a side-chain containing a detector and gain computer»; «Side-chain circuitry
  examines the input signal (or a separate Key Input) and issues a control voltage to adjust the gain of the
  signal path»; «The only difference between a compressor, limiter, AGC, de-esser, ducker, or gate, is the type
  of side-chain detector, the gain computer attributes and the type of gain control element used».
- Rango dinámico (sirve también al tema 1). RN155: «The dynamic range of an audio passage is the ratio of the
  loudest signal to the quietest signal»; «Professional-grade signal processing equipment can output maximum
  levels of +26 dBu, with the best noise floors being down around -94 dBu. This gives a dynamic range of 120 dB
  -- an impressive number coinciding nicely with the 120 dB dynamic range of normal human hearing» (cifras del
  fabricante).
- Expansor. RN155 cap. 5: «Expanders complement compressors by increasing (expanding) the dynamic range of the
  signal passing through it, i.e., an expander is a compressor running in reverse»; «Unlike a compressor, the
  expander reduces gain for signals below the threshold»; «an expander makes the quiet parts quieter»; «The
  term downward expander (or downward expansion) evolved to describe this type of application. The most common
  use is noise reduction». Ejemplo: umbral «just below the quietest recorded vocal level», ratio 2:1, escalón de
  −10 dB al ruido de fondo → «a noise reduction improvement of 10 dB».
- Puerta. RN155: «A gate is to an expander, as a limiter is to a compressor. Like an expander, gain is reduced
  below the threshold. Like a limiter, a gate must respond very quickly to changes in level, dictating the use of
  a peak detector in the side-chain. Unlike an expander, a gate uses a fixed ratio of infinity:1 and a variable
  depth»; «A gate is typically used to remove background noise between louder sounds»; «A gate is the extreme
  case of downward expansion». «When the incoming audio signal drops below the threshold point, the gate
  prevents further output by reducing the gain to "zero." Typically, this means attenuating all signals by about
  80 dB». «Common terminology refers to the gate "opening" and "closing."»
- Analogía que conviene citar: RN155 «a peak limiter is to a compressor as a noise gate is to an expander».

### 5.2 Parámetros de la puerta

- Umbral. RN155: «Threshold … is the beginning point of gain adjustment»; «A good expander extends the range to
  -60 dBu for low-level signals» (compresores «-40 dBu to +20 dBu»).
- Ataque. RN155: «For expanders (with ducking & gate features) this range changes to 0 ms ("instantaneous"
  attack …) to 250 ms»; «In gate mode, attack time determines how quickly the gate opens once the control signal
  exceeds the threshold setting».
- Mantenimiento (hold). RN155: «Hold · Provided by professional gates, with a typical range of 0 to 3 seconds.
  The hold time determines how long the gate remains open after the control signal drops below the threshold
  setting».
- Liberación. RN155: «In gate mode, the release time determines how quickly the gate closes as the control
  signal drops below the threshold setting». Rango típico (compresor y expansor): «between 25 ms and 2 seconds».
- Profundidad / rango (depth). RN155: «Depth · Provided on all gates, this control has a typical range of 0 to
  -80 dB. The depth control determines how many dB the signal is attenuated when the control signal is at or
  below the threshold setting».
- Clave externa y filtro de cadena lateral. RN155: «Almost all gates provide side-chain equalization and external
  Key Input. A good gate is able to "look-ahead" by delaying the main signal a small amount. The best gate
  combines look-ahead with pre-ramping».
- Histéresis: NO aparece en RN155; no se ha encontrado fuente primaria en esta sesión. Si se menciona, como
  oficio.

### 5.3 Usos y defectos

- Usos. RN155: «Gates find use in live sound to reduce crosstalk (bleed) from adjacent microphones, to keep toms
  from ringing … and to tighten up the sound. Gates are also used to punch up and tighten percussive instruments
  & drums. And gates control unwanted noise, such as preventing open microphones and hot instrument pick-ups from
  introducing extraneous sounds». Expansor en directo: «to reduce stage noise between passages for a quiet
  vocalist».
- Defectos. RN155: «poorly designed gates cause breathing and clicking. The term breathing describes an audible
  problem caused by hearing the noise floor rise and fall»; «Clicking is caused by opening the gate too fast. It
  is a common myth that if you make a gate open faster it will sound better; however, such is not the case. The
  faster a gate opens, the higher in frequency the click is».
- Ducker (enlaza con el ducking de edicion-montaje/03). RN155: «In ducker mode, attack time determines how
  quickly the signal is reduced as the control signal exceeds the threshold setting»; «The hold time determines
  how long the signal remains ducked when the control signal drops below the threshold setting»; depth «0 to -80
  dB … attenuated when the control signal is at or above the threshold setting».
- Limitador de picos (refuerza RTVE 07 §3). RN155: «Peak Limiter Uses · Prevent clipping and distortion in power
  amplifiers. Protection of loudspeakers …. Preventing overs (digital clipping) during recording. Preventing
  overmodulation of the transmitted signal in broadcast».

## Tema 13 · Medición y sonoridad

El tema RTVE 14 declara que **no consultó** el texto de R 128 y da como «de uso universal» −23 LUFS,
−1 dBTP, ±1 LU, 400 ms y 3 s. Ahora quedan todas confirmadas en fuente primaria, con matices.

### 13.1 Qué es la R 128 y su versión vigente

- Título y estado. R128, portada: «R 128 LOUDNESS NORMALISATION AND PERMITTED MAXIMUM LEVEL OF AUDIO
  SIGNALS · Status: EBU Recommendation · Geneva · November 2023». Es de la **UER/EBU (Unión Europea de
  Radiodifusión)**, no de la AES (el RTVE ya lo avisaba).
- Historia de versiones. R128, p. 2: «First published February 2010 · Revised August 2011 V2; relative gate
  changed from −8 to −10 LU · June 2014 V3; Target level (−23 LUFS) tolerance changed to ± 0.5 LU (except
  Live-programmes) · August 2020 V4; Tolerances updates and references to R 128 s1 and s2 added · November
  2023 V5; References to R 128 s3, s4 and Tech 3401 added».
- Planteamiento. R128, p. 3: «The EBU recommends the measurement of the average loudness of a programme
  ('Programme Loudness') for the normalisation of audio signals. The 'Maximum True Peak Level' of an audio
  signal should be used to check compliance with the upper technical limit of the signal chain. The measures
  'Loudness Range', 'Maximum Momentary Loudness' and 'Maximum Short-term Loudness' can be used to further
  characterise an audio signal…».
- Por qué (considerandos). R128, p. 3: «a) that peak normalisation of audio signals has led to considerable
  loudness differences between programmes and between broadcast channels; b) that the resulting loudness
  inconsistencies … are the cause of the most viewer/listener complaints; c) that … the QPPM (Quasi-Peak
  Programme Meter) specified in EBU Tech 3205-E does not reflect the loudness of an audio signal…; d) that
  … the permitted maximum level of an audio signal specified in ITU-R BS.645 is no longer appropriate; e) that
  measuring audio programme loudness has been defined in ITU-R BS.1770, introducing the measures LU (Loudness
  Unit) and LUFS (Loudness Units, referenced to Full Scale)». Sirve para sustentar el epígrafe RTVE «Por qué
  la sonoridad y no el pico» con fuente.

### 13.2 Las cifras (recomienda g–m)

- Objetivo. R128 h): «the Programme Loudness Level shall be normalised to a Target Level of −23.0 LUFS.
  Where attaining the Target Level is not achievable practically (for example, live programmes), a tolerance
  of ±1.0 LU is permitted. A broadcaster should ensure that a deviation from the Target Level towards the
  limits of the tolerance does not become standard practice».
- **Aviso de exactitud para el redactor**: en la V5 vigente ya NO aparece la tolerancia general de ±0,5 LU
  (que introdujo la V3 de 2014 según el historial); el texto vigente sólo da ±1,0 LU «where attaining the
  Target Level is not achievable practically (for example, live programmes)» y ±0,2 LU de medida. La tabla
  RTVE («Tolerancia en directo ±1 LU») es correcta; no añadir ±0,5 LU como vigente (error 7).
- Tolerancia de medida. R128 i): «for the implementation of Loudness workflows (for example, in Quality
  Control environments) a tolerance of ±0.2 LU is allowed in order to take account of measurement errors».
- Objetivo más bajo a propósito. R128 j): «in special cases the Programme Loudness Level may be normalised
  to a Target Level lower than −23.0 LUFS on purpose. This exception shall be clearly indicated to ensure
  that such a lower Programme Loudness Level is not compensated».
- Medidor. R128 k): «the measurement shall be made with a loudness meter compliant with ITU-R BS.1770
  (including the level-gating method described in equation (7)) and EBU Tech 3341».
- Todo el programa. R128 l): «the audio signal shall generally be measured in its entirety, without emphasis
  on specific foreground elements such as speech, music or sound effects».
- Pico real. R128 m): «the True Peak Level of a programme shall not exceed −1 dBTP (dB True Peak) during

- LUFS = LKFS. R128, nota 1: «LUFS' is equivalent to 'LKFS' (which is used in ITU-R BS.1770). The EBU uses
  'LUFS' which is compliant with international naming conventions».
- LRA y máximos. R128 n): «the measure Loudness Range (measured in compliance with EBU Tech 3342) may be used
  to evaluate the loudness variation of a programme, its potential subsequent dynamic treatment and the

  recommended due to too few data points (Loudness Range is based on the Short-term-Loudness values

- Metadatos. R128 p): «Loudness Metadata shall correctly indicate the actual Programme Loudness».
- Suplementos (útil para «qué se puede preguntar»). R128 q)–v): «short-form content (adverts; promos etc.)

- Máximos. T3341: «The loudness meter shall be able to display the maximum value of the 'Momentary Loudness'

- La puerta (I). T3341 §2.3: «The 'Integrated Loudness' shall be measured using the gating function specified

### 13.4 Rango de sonoridad (Tech 3342)

### 13.5 Medidores clásicos, alineación y margen (Tech 3343)

- Tono de alineación. T3343 §8.1: «An Alignment Signal in broadcasting consists of a sine-wave signal at a

- Fin del PML −9 dBFS. T3343 §8.1: «with the change to the "Maximum Permitted True-Peak Level" (−1 dBTP in

- Tono en el medidor de sonoridad. T3343 §8.1: «The alignment level of −18 dBFS (1 kHz tone) will read as −18

### 13.6 El algoritmo de sonoridad y el pico real (UIT-R BS.1770-5)

- Versión vigente: BS.1770-5 (11/2023). Título: «Algorithms to measure audio programme loudness and
  true-peak audio level». (UIT = Unión Internacional de Telecomunicaciones; UIT-R, su sector de
  radiocomunicaciones.)
- Alcance (recomienda 1-3). BS1770: «when an objective measure of the loudness of an audio channel or
  programme, produced with up to five main channels per Recommendation ITU-R BS.775 (mono source, stereo and
  3/2 multichannel sound), is required …, the algorithm specified in Annex 1 should be used»; para más
  canales «(such as the channel configurations specified in Recommendation ITU-R BS.2051) … Annex 3»; para
  «object-based audio signals or combination of channel- and object-based audio signals … Annex 4».
- Por qué el pico real. BS1770, considerando: «that digital media overloads abruptly, and thus even momentary
  overload should be avoided»; «that peak signal levels may increase due to commonly applied processes such as
  filtering or bit-rate reduction»; «that existing metering technologies do not reflect the true-peak level
  contained in a digital signal since the true-peak value may occur between samples».
- Las cuatro etapas. BS1770 Anexo 1: «The algorithm consists of four stages: – "K" frequency weighting; – mean
  square calculation for each channel; – channel-weighted summation (surround channels have larger weights,
  and the LFE channel is excluded); – gating of 400 ms blocks (overlapping by 75%), where two thresholds are
  used: – the first at −70 LKFS; – the second at −10 dB relative to the level measured after application of the
  first threshold».
- Ponderación K. BS1770 nota 1: «The K-weighting filter is composed of two stages of filtering; a first stage
  shelving filter and a second stage high-pass filter». «The first stage of the pre-filtering accounts for the
  acoustic effects of the head, where the head is modelled as a rigid sphere». «The frequency weighting in this
  measure, which is generated by the pre-filter (concatenation of the stage 1 filter to compensate for the
  acoustics effects of the head, and the stage 2 filter, the RLB weighting) is designated K-weighting». RLB =
  «revised low-frequency B-curve».
- LKFS. BS1770: «This designation signifies: Loudness, K-weighted, relative to nominal full scale. The LKFS
  unit is equivalent to a decibel in that an increase in the level of a signal by 1 dB will cause the loudness
  reading to increase by 1 LKFS». «If a 0 dB FS, 1 kHz (997 Hz to be exact …) sine wave is applied to the left,
  centre, or right channel input, the indicated loudness will equal −3.01 LKFS». Fórmula: «Loudness, LK =
  –0.691 + 10 log10 Σ Gi·zi»; «The constant −0.691 in equation (2) cancels out the K-weighting gain for 997 Hz».
- Pesos por canal (Tabla 3). BS1770: «Left (GL) 1.0 (0 dB); Right (GR) 1.0 (0 dB); Centre (GC) 1.0 (0 dB);
  Left surround (GLs) 1.41 (~ +1.5 dB); Right surround (GRs) 1.41 (~ +1.5 dB)». Por qué: T3343 nota 3: «The
  +1.5 dB weighting coefficient for the surround signals … has psychoacoustic reasons. Humans appear to perceive
  direct sounds coming from the back louder than frontal ones with the same sound pressure level».
- Límite. BS1770: «the algorithm is not, in general, suitable for use to estimate the subjective loudness of
  pure tones». Y nota 1: «measured loudness is an estimation of subjective loudness and involves some degree of
  uncertainty».
- Bloques. BS1770: «A gating block is a set of contiguous audio samples of duration Tg = 400 ms … The overlap of
  each gating block shall be 75% of the gating block duration». «The relative threshold Γr is calculated by
  measuring the loudness using the absolute threshold, Γa = –70 LKFS, and subtracting 10 from the result».
- Medidor de pico real (Anexo 2). BS1770: «The first step consists of imposing an attenuation of 12.04 dB
  (2-bit shift)»; «The 4 × over-sampling filter increases the sampling rate of the signal from 48 kHz to 192
  kHz»; «for an incoming signal at 96 kHz sample rate a 2 × over-sampling would be sufficient»; «The "dB TP"
  designation signifies decibels relative to 100% full scale, true-peak measurement». Infralectura: «a 3 dB
  under-read for an unfortunately-phased tone at a quarter of the sampling frequency».

### 13.7 Medidores de pico, vúmetro y fase (lo que el RTVE daba sin fuente)

- PPM EBU (QPPM). T3205: «In the first condition (normal mode) it is a quasi-peak-reading instrument having a
  nominal integration time of 10 ms»; «The integration time in normal mode shall be 10 ±2 ms»; retorno: «the
  indication shall fall to the -12 mark in 2.8 ±0.3 s in the normal [mode]»; escala: «twelve approximately
  equal 2 dB divisions»; «"Test" … 0 dB, referred to 0.775 V … This level is 9 dB below the maximum amplitude»;
  tipos IEC: «The I.E.C. recognizes three types of peak-programme meters, namely types I, IIa and IIb … the
  latter corresponds to the standard E.B.U. meter» (IEC Publication 268-10). Estado: la portada dice «This
  Technical Document is now superseded by EBU R128» (legacy). → Sustenta «el picómetro es rápido pero no llega
  al pico real» (cuasipico, 10 ms).
- Vúmetro. SOS (prensa técnica): «The bespoke moving-coil meter's relatively slow (300ms) needle rise and fall
  times ensured the meter would register low-frequency and sustained sounds better than brief transient peaks»;
  «a steady tone of +4dBm at the input … gave an indication of 0VU»; «The SVI's core specification lives on
  today, virtually unchanged, as IEC 60268-17:1990 Sound System Equipment. Part 17: Standard Volume
  Indicators». La norma IEC 60268-17 NO se ha leído (de pago): los 300 ms quedan con fuente secundaria.
- Pico de muestra frente a pico real: ver BS1770 considerando b) y Anexo 2 (13.6).
- Correlación de fase («fases» del enunciado). RTW: «Mostly, phase correlation is used to determine the mono
  compatibility of a stereo signal, but depending on where you use it, it can also reveal other things, such as

  linear scale going from -1 (switched polarity) over 0 (unrelated) to 1 (identical)»; «Normal stereo mixes
  usually show correlation values between 0.3 and 0.7» (cifra de fabricante, dar como tal); multicanal: «The
  Multicorrelator is a very useful tool when you work with surround sound and wanting to make sure that your
  surround mix also sounds great when down mixed to stereo or even mono». Salvedad: la frase de RTW «unrelated
  signals, the phase correlation between them will be 90 degrees» es una simplificación del fabricante; no

- Goniómetro/vectorscopio: no se encontró fuente primaria leíble en esta sesión (sólo Wikipedia y blogs). Hueco

- Control de calidad (QC): R128 i) da la tolerancia de medida ±0,2 LU «in Quality Control environments»; T3343

## Tema 14 · Audio multicanal, Dolby, 5.1, estéreo, mono, downmix y compatibilidad

El tema RTVE 15 declara que no consultó Dolby ni especificaciones de matriz, y da el LFE «hasta unos 120 Hz»
como orden de magnitud. Con BS.775-4 y Tech 3343 quedan confirmados con fuente el sistema 3/2, la
colocación de altavoces, el LFE, los coeficientes de downmix y la preferencia Lo/Ro.

### 14.1 El sistema de referencia 3/2 (5.1) · BS.775-4

- Objeto. BS775 resumen: «Recommendation ITU-R BS.775 recommends one universal multichannel stereophonic sound
  system with three front channels and two rear/side channels together with an optional low frequency effects
  (LFE) channel».
- Compatibilidad como principio. BS775, considerando: HDTV «should be capable of giving … compatibility with
  two-channel stereophonic and monophonic listening»; «that a hierarchy of compatible sound systems for
  broadcasting, cinema and recordings is useful for programme exchange and up- and down-mixing».
- Disposición de altavoces (recomienda 2). BS775: «three front loudspeakers combined with two rear/side
  loudspeakers»; «the left and right frontal loudspeakers are placed at the extremities of an arc subtending
  60° at the reference listening point» (es decir, ±30°); «both side/rear loudspeakers should be placed within
  the sectors from 100° to 120° from the centre front reference. Precise location is not necessary. Side/rear
  loudspeakers should be no closer to the listener than the frontal loudspeakers, unless compensating time delay
  is introduced»; «the acoustic centre of frontal loudspeakers should ideally be at a height approximately equal
  to that of the listener's ears». Si la base frontal es recta, «it may be necessary to introduce compensating
  time delays in the signal feed of the centre loudspeaker». Más de dos traseros: «disposed symmetrically and at
  equal intervals on the arc which measures from 60° to 150°».
- Señales (recomienda 3). BS775: «five reference recording/transmission signals for left (L), right (R), centre
  (C), channels for the front, and left surround (LS) and right surround (RS) channels for the side/rear.
  Additionally the system may include a low frequency effects signal …». «the LS and RS signals can be combined
  with one (mono surround, MS) or zero rear/side signals».
- Notación. BS775 palabras clave: «5.1, 2/0 channel format, 3/2 channel format»; Tabla 2 da los formatos «1/0;
  2/0; 3/0; 2/1; 3/1; 2/2» (delanteros/traseros). Mono = 1/0; estéreo = 2/0.
- Requisitos. BS775 Anexo 2: «Downward compatibility with sound systems providing lower number of channels (down
  to stereophonic and monophonic sound systems) shall be maintained»; «Real-time mixing for live broadcast shall
  be practicable».

### 14.2 El canal LFE («.1»)

- Banda. BS775 recomienda 4-5: «the LFE channel should be band-limited to its nominal frequency band (up to 120
  Hz)»; «broadcast of any television programme that contains an LFE channel should not transmit any information
  on that channel above the nominal 120 Hz cutoff frequency». Anexo 7: «The LFE channel should be capable of
  handling signals in the range of 20-120 Hz». → Corrige el «unos 120 hercios … como orden de magnitud» del
  RTVE: es cifra de norma (120 Hz).
- Nivel. BS775 Anexo 7: «The LFE channel is recorded with a level offset of −10 dB for the recording and
  exchange of multichannel sound programme material. This offset is compensated for in the reproduction system,
  where the LFE loudspeaker has an acoustic output (within its low frequency passband) of +10 dB with respect to
  the other channels». Nota: «the music industry, such as DVD-Audio or Super Audio CD, is currently coding the
  LFE channel such that zero offset gain is required on reproduction».
- Es un realce, no esencial. BS775 Anexo 7: «The LFE channel should not, however, be used for the entire low
  frequency content …»; «The LFE channel is often not included in a 2-channel downmix. The main channels must
  contain all the essential programme elements necessary for the audience»; «it is better to think of it only as
  an enhancement – and definitely not as an essential component of the audio programme»; «As most television
  programmes do not need to convey very high levels of low frequency energy, in general the LFE channel does not
  need to be employed». «Although LFE stands for "low frequency effects" in this Recommendation, in other
  standards it may be described as "low frequency enhancement"».
- LFE ≠ subwoofer. BS775 Anexo 7 Ap. 1: «There is often considerable misunderstanding about the use of the ".1"
  in surround sound, and how it relates to subwoofers»; «The purpose of a subwoofer is to extend to lower
  frequencies the response of a loudspeaker»; «domestic installations, where bass management is used …». Del
  Dolby AC-3: «it has a limited bandwidth, of only 120 Hz, and it has 10 dB of gain applied on reproduction. The
  five normal channels in Dolby AC-3 are full band-width».
- Sonoridad: el LFE no cuenta (BS1770 y T3343 §4.2, ver 13.5-13.6).

### 14.3 Downmix y compatibilidad

- Coeficientes UIT (BS775 Tabla 2, «Downward mixing equations for 3/2 source material»):
  - Mono 1/0: «C = 0.7071 L + 0.7071 R + 1.0000 C + 0.5000 LS + 0.5000 RS».
  - Estéreo 2/0: «L = 1.0000 L + 0.7071 C + 0.7071 LS»; «R = 1.0000 R + 0.7071 C + 0.7071 RS».
  - 3/0: «L = 1.0000 L + 0.7071 LS», «R = 1.0000 R + 0.7071 RS», «C = 1.0000 C».
  (0,7071 = −3 dB; 0,5 = −6 dB: equivalencia aritmética, no dicha en la fuente.)
- EBU sobre coeficientes por defecto. T3343 §7.1: «In the case of missing or unreliable Downmix Metadata, a good
  starting point is to look at the coefficients described in ITU-R BS.775-2: L, R front: 0 dB; C, LS, RS: −3 dB».
- Lo/Ro y Lt/Rt. T3343 §7.1: «There exist two different Downmix methods in a receiver: Lo/Ro (Left only/Right
  only) which directly combines the channels according to the downmix coefficients, and Lt/Rt (Left total/Right
  total) which applies ±90° phase shifting to a Mono-sum of the surround channels in order to achieve better
  compatibility with matrix-surround playback systems (for example, Dolby ProLogic)». → Da con fuente el
  mecanismo que el RTVE describía «de forma conceptual».
- Preferencia EBU. T3343: «Mainly due to the artefacts of Matrix-Surround systems, an Lt/Rt-Downmix is even more
  unpredictable as far as the resulting Loudness Level is concerned. Together with the general sonic alterations,
  this is the reason why the EBU recommends the Downmix method Lo/Ro as the default setting for the relevant
  Metadata parameter ("Preferred Downmix Method")».
- De qué depende la sonoridad del downmix. T3343: «The chosen Downmixing method (Lo/Ro or Lt/Rt); The actual
  downmix coefficients themselves (+3/+1.5/0/−1.5/−3/−4.5/−6/−[símbolo perdido en el volcado de texto; previsiblemente ∞, no comprobado en el PDF]); The programme content in the Centre and
  surround channels; The correlation between the channels and; Potential safety-limiting to avoid overload».
  «The weighting of the two surround signals is +1.5 dB in the algorithm specified in ITU-R BS.1770, but the
  default downmix coefficient for these signals is −3 dB! As a result, the difference in loudness terms
  regarding the surround signals of the two mixes is 4.5 dB by default!». Divergencia: «the Stereo-downmix of
  such a Surround-Sound mix can have an up to 3 LU higher loudness level!».
- Perfiles Dolby Digital. T3343: «Initially, when there was only one profile, the parameters were coarser, with
  −3/−4.5/−6 dB for the Centre and −3/−6/−[símbolo perdido; previsiblemente ∞] dB for the Surround channels. Now, Extended Bitstream Information
  (Extended BSI) provides the finer intermediate steps …; also DVB TS 101 154 downmix coefficients offer the same
  resolution …». «legacy decoders … would fall back to the fewer and coarser coefficients of profile 1».
- Evitar saturar. T3343: «Care should also be taken to avoid overload of the downmixed signal. This can be
  achieved with a dynamics processor upstream. Static scaling (overall level reduction) should be avoided».
- Upmix. T3343 §7.2: «An Upmix should never replace an original discrete multichannel audio mix, though».
- Metadatos Dolby. T3343 §6: «in the Dolby AC-3 Metadata system, these parameters are called dialnorm (dialogue
  normalisation), dynrng (dynamic range) and Centre/Surround Downmix Level»; «−27 (the factory default for
  dialnorm in the Dolby-Digital system) or −31 (the lowest possible value in that system)».

### 14.4 Dolby E (y Dolby ED2)

- Qué es. TREV: «Dolby E is a data-stream, designed by Dolby Laboratories, which carries up to eight channels
  of digital audio within a standard stereo channel (AES3), as well as transporting metadata which describes
  the audio and its reproduction. It is a professional data-stream, designed for use within production and
  broadcast infrastructures – but not for use as an emission codec or by consumers. It employs light data-rate
  reduction, ensuring that multiple encode-decode cycles are possible».
- Generaciones. RDD19 §3: «a method of reducing the data rate of PCM (baseband) audio signals while preserving
  the subjective quality of the audio through up to ten encode – decode cycles. The data rate reduction ratio
  allows up to eight audio signals to be carried in a single AES3 digital audio stream».
- Dolby E y Dolby Digital. TREV: «Dolby E is often used in conjunction with Dolby Digital, which is a consumer
  data-stream, also carrying multiple channels of audio (up to six) and associated metadata, and is used as the
  emission codec by many broadcasters. The consumer metadata used in a Dolby Digital stream is carried by the
  Dolby E stream … allowing metadata continuity from studio to home».
- Tramas y banda de guarda. TREV: «Dolby E divides audio into frames at a rate aligned with the associated
  video. A Dolby E frame may not be split or modified in any way – such as gain adjustment, sample-rate
  conversion or equalisation. An incomplete or corrupt frame will not reproduce the intended audio, but will
  likely cause a mute for the duration of the frame or be interpreted as PCM audio, causing a loud audible
  "splat" in the output. Each frame is slightly shorter than a video frame, allowing a guard band to be used …
  The switching point used by mixers and routers falls in this guard band, allowing switching between video
  sources without the corruption of the embedded Dolby E». RDD19: «The guard bands between blocks are
  co-located with the Vertical Interval Switch points of the associated video signal».
- Retardo. TREV: «Dolby E encode and decode cycles each incur a fixed delay of 1 video frame (e.g. 40 ms in a
  25 Hz system)». Opciones de entrega: «In-Sync Encoded» y «Advanced (decode-compensated) – The encoded audio
  appears one frame ahead of the video, meaning that after the decode delay, the audio is in sync». Aviso:
  equipos «Dolby-E-aware, "helpfully" include a frame of video delay to compensate for the audio decoding delay.
  Such devices include de-embedders, MPEG video encoders».
- Mezclar exige decodificar. TREV: «while Dolby E's frame-synchronous nature allows cut transitions without
  decoding, actions such as mixing audio and adding voiceovers requires careful implementation of a
  decode-process-encode cycle».
- Vídeo progresivo 50/60. RDD19: «Dolby E encoders generate data blocks that are synchronized with video frame
  rates of up to 30 frames per second»; con progresivo a 50 o 60 «the Dolby E data stream may only be switched
  on a Progressive frame … boundary that occurs during the Dolby E guard band intervals, or in other words, on
  every second P Frame boundary».
- Dolby ED2 (inmersivo). ED2: «Dolby ED2 is an extension to the Dolby E codec to support immersive audio»;
  «Backward compatible with Dolby E pass-through and decoding products»; «Supports up to 16 audio channels with
  8 channels carried per Dolby ED2 substream on a single AES3/SDI audio pair»; «While Dolby E supports surround
  sound audio and the carriage of multiple streams, it does not have the extensions necessary to carry Dolby
  Atmos audio, loudness information, and other important metadata».
- NO confirmado en fuente primaria: los modos de 16/20/24 bits y «16-bit mode has a maximum of 6 channels» (sólo
  en guías de terceros —Hybrik, Wikipedia—; el portal de documentación de Dolby dio 503). No darlo.

### 14.5 Canales frente a objetos: la cama de Dolby Atmos

- ATMOS (v3.0, 2018): «Bed tracks are audio tracks with bed material. In a basic Dolby Atmos setup, this is a
  7.1.2 multichannel track»; «Object tracks are audio tracks with object audio»; «The Dolby Atmos Renderer
  supports up to 118 mono objects, or a combination of mono and stereo objects totaling up to 118 object channel
  paths»; «the workstation receives up to 128 input audio tracks»; plantillas: «configured for a Dolby Atmos mix
  using one 7.1.2 bed and up to 118 mono objects». (7.1.2 = 10 canales; 10 + 118 = 128: aritmética propia.)
  Posicionamiento: «Positioning for beds is based on the width of the multichannel bed in the DAW. Positioning
  for objects is based on Dolby Atmos metadata».
- Esto confirma con fuente de fabricante el «7.1.2 como cama» que el RTVE apoyaba sólo en la plantilla.
  Salvedad: la guía leída es la v3.0 de 2018; no se ha comprobado si versiones posteriores del Renderer cambian
  la cifra. Decir «en la guía del Renderer» y no «máximo absoluto».
- Normas UIT de configuraciones y objetos: BS.1770-5 remite a «the channel configurations specified in
  Recommendation ITU-R BS.2051» y mide objetos en su Anexo 4. BS.2051 y BS.2076 (ADM) no se han leído: sólo
  consta su existencia por la cita de BS.1770.


## Lo que no se pudo confirmar (huecos declarados)

1. Normas IEC y AES de pago, no leídas: IEC 60268-4 (micrófonos), IEC 60268-17 (vúmetro, 300 ms), IEC 60268-10
   (PPM tipos I/IIa/IIb; sólo por la mención de Tech 3205), IEC 61938 (phantom), AES48 (masas). Sus cifras van
   atribuidas al fabricante que las cita.
2. Phantom: 6,81 kΩ por rama y «44-52 V» sólo en webs de terceros; no darlos como cita.
3. Dolby E: modos de 16/20/24 bits y «6 canales a 16 bits» sólo en terceros (portal Dolby dio HTTP 503).
4. Dolby Atmos: cama 7.1.2 y 118 objetos confirmados en la guía del Renderer v3.0 (2018); no comprobado en
   versiones posteriores.
5. BS.2051, BS.2076 (ADM), EBU Tech 3344, R 128 s1-s4, Tech 3401: sólo consta su existencia y objeto (citados por
   R 128 o BS.1770-5), no su contenido.
6. Goniómetro/vectorscopio e histéresis de la puerta: sin fuente primaria en esta sesión; si se mencionan, como
   oficio.
7. Principio físico del micrófono de cinta: no leído en fuente primaria.
8. Norma de control de calidad de ficheros (EBU QC): no buscada.

## Correcciones al material RTVE que el redactor debe aplicar

- Tema 13 (RTVE 14): quitar la declaración «el texto de la recomendación EBU R 128 no se ha volcado»; ahora está
  leída (R 128-2023). Las cifras −23 LUFS, −1 dBTP, ±1 LU, 0,4 s y 3 s se confirman; añadir ±0,2 LU de medida,
  −70 LUFS y −10 LU de puerta de I, −20 LU de LRA, escalas EBU +9/+18, M y S sin puerta. No presentar ±0,5 LU como
  vigente. El «−1 dBTP» es «during production (linear audio)».
- Tema 13: el PML −9 dBFS (BS.645) está obsoleto según T3343; el PPM EBU Tech 3205 está «superseded by EBU R128».
- Tema 14 (RTVE 15): el LFE «hasta unos 120 Hz como orden de magnitud» es cifra de norma (BS.775-4: «up to 120
  Hz»); la cama 7.1.2 ya no descansa «sólo en la plantilla» (Dolby Renderer Guide); el Lt/Rt ya tiene mecanismo
  con fuente (T3343: «±90° phase shifting to a Mono-sum of the surround channels»); la EBU recomienda Lo/Ro por
  defecto.
- Tema 2 (RTVE 11): «48 V —también 24 y 12—» se puede anclar a IEC 61938:2018 vía DPA, con ±4 V, 7 mA nominal y
  10 mA máximo.
- Tema 1 y 3: la regla «polaridad invertida = 180°» debe matizarse (DPA-PPD: no es exactamente un desfase).
