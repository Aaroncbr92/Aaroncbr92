## Tema 12 · Radiofrecuencia aplicada a microfonía inalámbrica

### 12.1 El CNAF vigente · Orden TDF/732/2026 (BOE 17-07-2026)

- Base legal (preámbulo): «La Ley 11/2022, de 28 de junio, General de Telecomunicaciones, establece en su artículo 85 que el espectro radioeléctrico es un bien de dominio público, cuya titularidad y administración corresponden al Estado.» El CNAF lo aprueba el Ministerio según «el artículo 6 del Reglamento de desarrollo de la Ley 11/2022 [...] aprobado por el Real Decreto 123/2017, de 24 de febrero».
- Artículo único: «Mediante la presente orden se aprueba el Cuadro Nacional de Atribución de Frecuencias (CNAF), cuyo texto se inserta como anexo a la presente orden.»
- DA 2.ª: «El Secretario de Estado de Telecomunicaciones e Infraestructuras Digitales autorizará usos de carácter temporal o experimental distintos de los previstos por el Cuadro Nacional de Atribución de Frecuencias (CNAF), siempre que no causen perturbaciones [...]». (Vía para usos temporales fuera del cuadro.)
- Disposición derogatoria única: «Queda derogada la Orden ETD/1449/2021, de 16 de diciembre, por la que se aprueba el Cuadro Nacional de Atribución de Frecuencias».
- DF 2.ª: «entrará en vigor el día siguiente al de su publicación» → **vigente desde el 18-07-2026**.
- Título competencial: «artículo 149.1.21.ª de la Constitución Española» (DF 1.ª).

### 12.3 Antenas · Shure, *Wireless Systems Guide for Antenna Setup* (G. Sigismondi y C. Tapia, «A Shure Educational Publication», © 2013/2016)
Fuente: https://www.shure.com/damfiles/default/global/documents/publications/en/performance-production/wireless-systems-guide-for-antenna-setup-english.pdf-6a0564ec711b3449986ddf9ce5951a9a.pdf (volcado `fuentes/canal-sur/sonido/shure-antenna.txt`). Documentación de fabricante: dato de oficio, no norma.

- Tamaño: «The size of an antenna is directly related to the wavelength of the frequency to be received. The most common types used in wireless audio systems are 1/4-wave and 1/2-wave omni-directional antennas, and unidirectional antennas.»
- Cálculo: «Wavelength for radio signals can be calculated by dividing the speed of light by frequency». Ejemplo: «a 200 MHz wave has a wavelength of approximately 6 feet (2 m). Therefore, a 1/2-wave receiver antenna would be about 3 feet (1 m) long, and a 1/4-wave antenna would be about 18 inches (45 cm).» «For a system operating at 500 MHz, a 1/4-wave antenna should be about 6 inches (15 cm).»
- λ/4 y plano de tierra: «These antennas require a ground plane for proper reception [...]. Do not use a 1/4-wave antenna for remote antenna mounting.» «A 1/2-wave antenna does not require a ground plane, making it suitable for remote mounting in any location. While there is a theoretical gain of about 3 dB over a 1/4-wave antenna, in practice, this benefit is seldom realized.»
- Direccionales: «unidirectional, such as yagi or log periodic antennas [...] can provide up to 10 dB more gain than a 1/4-wave antenna, and can also reject interfering sources from other directions by as much as 30 dB. Yagi antennas are rarely used in wireless microphone applications due to their quite narrow bandwidth». «The log periodic antenna achieves greater bandwidth by using multiple dipoles whose size and spacing vary in a logarithmic progression.» Montaje: «with the transverse elements in the vertical direction, rather than horizontal as in a television application, because the transmitting antennas are usually also vertical.» «A minimum distance of 50 feet (15 m) is recommended between transmitter and unidirectional antennas.»
- Conectores: «BNC connections are most often used for antenna inputs».
- Visión directa: «always try to maintain line of sight between the receiving and transmitting antennas». «Metal equipment racks will block RF from reaching the antennas mounted inside.»
- Separación: «Antennas should be separated from each other by a minimum of one quarter wavelength – about 16 inches (40 cm) for VHF units and about 4 inches (10 cm) for UHF units. [...] beyond one full wavelength the advantage becomes negligible.» Altura: por encima de «crowd level» (5 o 6 pies).
- Distribución: «Using a passive splitter results in a signal loss of about 3 dB for every split. As a general rule, no more than 5 dB of loss is acceptable». Pasivo sólo para 2 receptores; «If distribution is needed for more than two systems, an active antenna distribution system is recommended.» Cascada: mejor «a “master” antenna distribution system to split the signal to a second tier of “slave” distribution systems». Guía: «2 receivers = passive antenna splitter»; «4 - 5 receivers = active antenna distribution».
- Cable: «50 ohm low loss cable is typically used in wireless microphone applications. Using 75 ohm cable results in additional loss». «A loss of between 3 and 5 dB of signal strength is considered acceptable. If the cable run results in a loss of greater than 5 dB, active antenna amplifiers must be used». Ganancia neta de amplificadores: «Net gain should be less than 10 dB.»
- Combinación: «A passive combiner will typically result in at least 3 dB of loss». Monitores in-ear: «Several closely-spaced, high-power transmitters suffer from excessive intermodulation [...]. a passive combiner should be used for combining two transmitters. For more than two, though, an active combiner is recommended.» «active antenna combiners should never be “actively” cascaded.»

### 12.4 Coordinación de frecuencias, intermodulación, diversidad · Shure, T. Vear, *Selection and Operation of Wireless Microphone Systems* («Updated for digital wireless», © 2014)
Fuente: https://www.shure.com/damfiles/default/global/documents/publications/en/performance-production/selection_and_operation_of_wireless_microphone_systems_english.pdf-2f464697de562ef1f5522b3d52602f48.pdf (volcado `shure-selection.txt`).

- Dos pasos: «Selecting the operating frequency of a wireless audio system is a two-step process: first, choose an appropriate radio frequency band; second, choose the appropriate operating frequency (or frequencies) within that band.» La coordinación «must take into account various factors including the physical (limitations), the mathematical (calculations) and the political (regulations).» Fabricantes: «pre-selected frequency groups» y «proprietary computer programs».
- Misma frecuencia: los receptores «cannot properly demodulate more than one signal on the same frequency»; si uno es más fuerte «it will "capture" the receiver».
- Separación mínima: «The recommended minimum frequency difference ranges from 300kHz to 1.5 MHz, depending on receiver selectivity.»
- IM: «it arises when signals are applied to "non-linear" circuits»; «Non-linear circuits are intrinsic to the design of wireless components and include the output stages of transmitters and the input stages of receivers.» «IM can occur when transmitters are in close proximity to each other» y «when transmitters are operated very close to receivers».
- Tercer orden: «The strongest IM products are the two so-called 3rd order products produced by two adjacent transmitters [...] IM1 = (2 x f1) – f2 / IM2 = (2 x f2) – f1». Ejemplo: «if f1 = 180MHz and f2 = 190MHz, then F = 10MHz. Thus, IM1 = 170MHz and IM2 = 200MHz.» Margen: «most manufacturers recommend a minimum margin of 250 kHz (0.25 MHz) between any 3rd order IM product and any operating frequency.» Complejidad: «a group of 10 wireless microphone systems involves thousands of calculations.»
- Frecuencias internas: oscilador local y FI; ejemplo «a typical intermediate frequency (IF) of 10.7 MHz, a receiver tuned to 200.7 MHz would have its LO operating at 190.0 MHz».
- Multitrayecto: «Cancellation of the signal can occur when the direct and indirect waves are similar in amplitude and opposite in phase.» Efectos: «"noise-up"», «"hits"», «"dropout"». «multi-path effects tend to be more severe in the UHF range.»
- Diversidad: «using multiple (usually two) antennas to take advantage of the very low probability of simultaneous dropouts at two different antenna locations»; «at least one-quarter wavelength separation between antennas is necessary [...] about 40 cm for VHF systems and about 10 cm for UHF systems». Tipos que describe: «passive antenna combining» («not a "true" diversity design»), «antenna phase diversity», «antenna switching diversity», receptor con conmutación y «receiver combining» («essentially an intelligent panpot, not a switch»; «Signal-to-noise is improved by up to 3 dB»). «The term "true" diversity has come to imply those systems which have two receiver sections».
- Squelch: «The function of this circuit is to mute or silence the audio output of the receiver in the absence of the desired radio signal.» Variantes: «noise squelch» y «"tone-key" or "tone-code" squelch».
- Compansión: existe el apartado «companding» (analógico); no se ha extraído cifra.

### 15.1 Dante: qué es un flujo, unicast y multicast · Audinate DC 4.18
- «Flow: Dante media routing creates flows. Each flow carries several channels of audio, or one channel of video from a transmitter to one or more receivers.»
- «Unicast flows are point-to-point from a single transmitter to a single receiver. Unicast flows typically have room for 4 channels of audio or 1 channel of video.» En otro pasaje: «a unicast flow typically assigns space for 4 channels of audio». → **confirma los 4 canales por flujo unicast** de RTVE (pregunta 47), con el matiz «typically».
- «Multicast flows are one-to-many from a single transmitter to any number of receivers. [...] Unlike unicast routing, multicast flows consume network bandwidth even if there are no receivers, but do not require additional bandwidth to add more receivers. Multicast flows can carry Dante audio, or RTP audio (AES67 or ST 2110-30).»
- «Dante routing is unicast by default.» «Dante receivers will automatically prefer multicast to unicast if it is available.» «In contrast to unicast flows, multicast flows must be set up on the transmitting Dante device before receivers can subscribe to these flows.»
- Suscripción: «Dante routing is performed by associating a receiving (Rx) channel with a transmitting (Tx) channel. This is called 'subscription'.» Formato «Audio L@Source».
- Formatos: «Devices can usually be switched between media formats, but will not support more than one at a time. It is only possible to set up a subscription between channels which have a common media format.»
- Conmutadores: «IGMP snooping must be enabled on network switches that will be carrying Dante video on 1Gbps» (vídeo); con IGMP bien configurado el multicast no inunda la red (nota hacia línea 3813). Causa de pérdida de sincronía citada: «Poorly-implemented EEE (Energy Efficient Ethernet)» y «Overloaded network links».
- QoS: «The DSCP is a field in the IP packet header used to classify and prioritise network traffic (Quality of Service, or QoS).» «EF is the default value for PTPv2 event packets on all Dante devices. This value is specified by the AES67 standard.» «PTP packets with a DSCP value of CS7 have higher priority those set to EF. This value is recommended for Dante flows.» (sic)

### 15.2 Dante: latencia · Audinate DC 4.18, apartado «Latency»
- «For Dante audio devices, variation in latency in the network is compensated for at the receiver. Each receiver has a device latency setting. This setting defines the latency between the timestamps on the incoming audio samples and when those samples are played out.»
- «The typical default latency for a Dante audio device is 1 msec. This is sufficient for a very large network, consisting of a Gigabit network core (with up to 10 hops between edge switches) and 100 megabit links to Dante devices.»
- «Smaller, Gigabit-only networks can use lower values of latency (down to 150 µsec for very fast devices, such as PCIe cards).» → **confirma los ~150 µs de RTVE**.
- «Latency is set on the receiver.» Negociación: «If the transmitter and receiver have different latency settings, Dante will use the higher of the two as the effective flow latency.» Ejemplo Ultimo: mínimo 1 ms.
- «Note: The minimum latency available for a device connected to a 100 Mbps network port is 1 msec. Using a latency lower than 1 msec over a 100 Mbps link will result in a subscription error, with the tooltip 'Tx Scheduler Failure'.»
- Corrección a RTVE: RTVE dice «el sistema entero se alinea al mayor». La fuente dice que la latencia efectiva **de cada flujo** es la mayor de emisor y receptor. No habla de alineación de todo el sistema. El redactor debe usar la de la fuente.

### 15.3 Dante: reloj PTP · Audinate DC 4.18
- «All Dante-enabled devices use the IEEE 1588 Precision Time Protocol (PTP) across the network to synchronize their local clocks to a leader clock, providing sample-accurate time alignment throughout the network.»
- Elección: «One Dante device will be elected as the PTP Leader Clock for the network; all other Dante devices act as a PTP Follower Clocks». «Devices with clock inputs (e.g. Word Clock or AES3) will be preferred in the election process. A gigabit connected device is preferred over a device connected via 100Mbps. A tie-breaker rule of the lowest MAC address is used». Se puede forzar con «'Preferred Leader'».
- Versiones: «Two versions of PTP exist: PTPv1 and PTPv2. By default, Dante devices use PTPv1. However, when RTP is enabled for a device, it also uses PTPv2». «AES67 and ST 2110-30 require PTPv2 (Precision Time Protocol, version 2)».
- Reloj externo: «'Enable Sync To External' will use the external word clock from its host equipment to tune its onboard VCXO.»
- Terminología: la guía usa ya «Leader/Follower» (antes master/slave).

### 15.4 Dante: redundancia · Audinate DC 4.18, «Redundancy»
- «Many Dante devices support redundant media routing. These devices have two network interfaces, named primary and secondary. [...] If redundancy is being used, secondary interfaces should be connected to a second separate network. Secondary interfaces cannot communicate with primary interfaces.»
- «The same media data is transmitted on both the primary and secondary networks simultaneously. In the event of a failure on one network, media will continue to flow via the other network.»
- «Dante redundancy requires that both the primary and secondary interfaces on any redundant device are connected using the same link speed.» «Dante devices that do not support redundancy must be connected to the primary network only.»
- Reloj en redundancia: «the clock synchronization protocol operates over both primary and secondary networks. Each network will have a designated PTP leader clock; usually this will be the same device on both networks.»

### 15.5 AES67 y ST 2110-30 · Audinate DC 4.18 y SMPTE ST 2110-30:2025
- Audinate: «AES67 is an open standard for audio-over-IP interoperability, allowing devices from different manufacturers to exchange audio streams.» «SMPTE ST 2110-30 is a broadcast industry standard for transporting audio over IP networks, building on AES67 with additional requirements for clocking and network configuration.» «Avoid mixing AES67 and ST 2110-30 modes on the same network unless you understand the implications for clocking and compatibility.»
- ST 2110-30:2025 alcance: «This standard specifies the real-time, RTP-based transport of PCM digital audio streams over IP networks by reference to AES67. An SDP-based signaling method is defined for metadata necessary to receive and interpret the stream.» «Non-PCM digital audio signals including compressed audio signals are outside the scope of this standard.»
- Referencia normativa: la ST 2110-30:2025 cita «AES67-2023 AES standard for audio applications of networks - High-performance streaming audio-over-IP interoperability» → **edición vigente de AES67 citada: 2023**.
- Muestreo (§6.1): «All senders and receivers of PCM digital audio conforming to this standard shall support a digital audio sampling rate of 48 kHz, and should support digital audio sampling rates of 44.1 kHz, or 96 kHz, or both. Other sampling rates are out of scope.» (Apoyo indirecto a los 96 kHz de la pregunta 52 de RTVE, pero es texto de la ST 2110-30, no de la AES67.)
- §6.2.1: «Digital audio streams shall conform to AES67, including the Session Description Protocol (SDP) as described in IETF RFC 8866». «digital audio receivers need not support Session Initiation Protocol (SIP)».
- Niveles (Tabla 2, emisores): A 48 kHz, 1000 µs, 1-8 canales; AX 96 kHz, 1000 µs, 1-4; B 48 kHz, 125 µs, 1-8; BX 96 kHz, 125 µs, 1-8; C 48 kHz, 125 µs, 9-64; CX 96 kHz, 125 µs, 9-32. «All senders and receivers shall be compliant to Level A as defined in this document.»

### 15.7 PTP perfil SMPTE · SMPTE ST 2059-2:2021 y ST 2059-1:2021
- Alcance 2059-2: «This standard specifies a Precision Time Protocol profile specifically for the synchronization of audio/video equipment in a professional broadcast environment. The SMPTE PTP profile is based on IEEE Std 1588-2008».
- Introducción: «All clocks ultimately derive their time from a clock known as the grandmaster clock.» Perfil PTP (cita de IEEE 1588-2008 §19.3.1.1): «A PTP profile is a set of required options, prohibited options, and the ranges and defaults of configurable attributes.»
- Identificador: «Profile identifier: 68-97-E8-00-01-00». BMCA por defecto de IEEE 1588-2008.
- §6.4: «The delay request-response mechanism shall be the default path delay measurement mechanism. The peer delay mechanism may also be implemented.»
- Valores por defecto (§6.5.2): priority1 y priority2 = 128 (0-255); «defaultDS.domainNumber Default value: 127», rango 0-127; logAnnounceInterval 0 (-3 a +1); announceReceiptTimeout 3 (2-10); «portDS.logSyncInterval: Default value: -3», rango -7 a -1 («the logarithm to base 2 of the time interval in seconds» → -3 = 1/8 s, 8 mensajes Sync por segundo: cálculo propio). «Followers shall support one-step and two-step clocks.»
- Términos: «leader [...] Referred to as ‘master’ in IEEE Std 1588-2008»; «follower [...] Referred to as ‘slave’».
- 2059-1 §6.1: «The SMPTE Epoch shall be 01 January 1970 00:00:00 TAI.» «Note 1: The SMPTE Epoch is the same as the PTP Epoch specified in IEEE Std 1588-2008.» Alcance: define la época, la alineación de señales y fórmulas para «SMPTE ST 12-1 Time Address values» (código de tiempo) desde datos PTP.
- Aviso: el texto de IEEE 1588 (2008 o 2019) no se ha leído (de pago). Lo que se afirma de PTP sale de SMPTE y de Audinate. La «precisión sub-microsegundo» de RTVE sigue sin fuente leída.

### 15.8 Redundancia sin cortes · SMPTE ST 2022-7:2019
- Alcance: «This standard defines requirements for multiple redundant streams of RTP packets to allow for the creation of a single reconstructed output stream through seamless protection switching at the RTP datagram level.»
- Introducción: «a standard for seamless reconstruction of a stream of RTP datagrams based on the transmission of multiple streams of identical content over potentially diverse paths». «a receiver could switch between them on a datagram by datagram basis without impact to the content or the stream.»
- §6: «The transmitter shall transmit at least two streams, each containing copies of each RTP datagram. The RTP header and the RTP payload shall be identical for each datagram copy.»
- Se aplica también a audio: definición 4.5 de flujo de entrada incluye como ejemplos «SMPTE ST 2110-30, SMPTE ST 2110-40 and AES67».
- Clases de receptor (Tabla 1), PD = diferencia máxima de retardo entre caminos: A (low-skew, intra-facility) PD ≤ 10 ms; B (moderate-skew, short-haul) ≤ 50 ms; C (high-skew, long-haul) ≤ 450 ms SBR / ≤ 150 ms HBR; D (ultra low-skew, «Physical Layer LAN Redundancy») ≤ 150 µs. SBR = «payload bit rate of less than 270 Mbit/s» (el audio cae ahí); HBR ≥ 270 Mbit/s.
- Diferencia con Dante (elaboración con las dos fuentes): Dante manda los mismos datos por la red primaria y la secundaria, y la ST 2022-7 fija cómo un receptor rehace un flujo RTP paquete a paquete desde dos copias. La guía de Audinate no dice que la redundancia Dante nativa sea ST 2022-7. No se afirma.

### 11.1 AES/EBU (AES3) · EBU Tech 3250 (3.ª ed., 2004)
- Alcance: «serial digital transmission of two channels of periodically sampled and linearly represented digital audio data in a broadcasting complex, up to a distance of a few hundred metres». «it is intended that the interface be primarily used at 48 kHz, as this is the recommended sampling frequency for use in broadcasting studios (CCIR Recommendation 646).»
- Estructura: «Each sub-frame is divided into 32 time slots, numbered from 0 to 31». Muestra: «Time slots 4 to 27 (audio samples word)», MSB en 27. Con 24 bits el LSB va en el slot 4; con 20 bits, «time slots 8-27», y los 4-7 quedan para otros usos (auxiliares). Bits 28 validez, 29 usuario, 30 estado de canal y 31 paridad.
- «A frame is uniquely composed of two sub-frames». «The block is a group of 192 consecutive frames.» Preámbulos X, Y y Z: Z «once every 192 frames» marca el inicio de bloque.
- Canales: «The left or "A" channel is in sub-frame 1 and the right or "B" channel is in sub-frame 2.»
- Codificación: «time slots 4 to 31 are encoded in biphase-mark», para que el interfaz sea «insensitive to the polarity of connections».
- Estado de canal: lleva p. ej. «length of audio sample words, number of audio channels, sampling frequency, time code, alphanumeric source and destination codes, and pre-emphasis». Errata de la fuente: pone «organised in 92-bit blocks, subdivided into 24 Bytes». 24 bytes son 192 bits y el apéndice 1 habla de «The channel status clock format of 192 bits». Hay que usar 192.
- Eléctrico (§6): basado en «ITU-T Recommendation V.11». Cable: «balanced and screened (shielded) with nominal characteristic impedance of 110 Ω». Emisor: «internal impedance of 110 Ω ± 20%». Amplitud: «between 2 and 7 V peak-to-peak, when measured across a 110 Ω resistor». Receptor: «110 Ω ± 20%». Nivel mínimo: diagrama de ojo con «Vmin of 200 mV». Aviso: «The application of more than one receiver to any one line might create transmission errors due to the resulting impedance mismatch.» Tampoco admite ecualización en emisión: «there shall be no equalization before transmission».
- Conector (§6.2.5): «the circular latching three-pin connector described in IEC 60268-12 (this type of connector is normally called "XLR")». Salida: macho en el equipo. Pines: «Pin 1: Cable shield or signal earth, Pin 2: Signal Pin 3: Signal». «(Note that the relative polarity of pins 2 and 3 is not important in the digital case).» Rotulado recomendado «"DI" and "DO"».
- Jitter intrínseco: «less then 0,025 UI» (sic).

### 11.2 AES3 por coaxial y BNC · ANSI/SMPTE 276M-1995
- Alcance: «a point-to-point coaxial cable interface for the transmission of AES/EBU digital audio signals throughout television production and broadcast facilities», compatible con «analog video equipment, such as nonclamping distribution amplifiers, switchers, cables, and connectors».
- Generador: «unbalanced output circuit with a source impedance of 75 ohms»; amplitud «1.0 V ± 10%, when measured across a 75-ohm resistive load».
- Cable: «nominal characteristic impedance of 75 ohms over the frequency range 0.1 MHz to 6.0 MHz». Conector: «BNC as described in IEC 169-8, but may feature an impedance of 75 ohms». Ecualización: «equalization shall be provided at the link receiver only». El anexo A (informativo) trata la conversión entre circuitos balanceados de 110 Ω y desequilibrados de 75 Ω.
- Nota: es de 1995. No se ha comprobado si hay revisión posterior en pub.smpte.org (sólo aparece «19951201-pub»).

### 11.3 MADI (AES10) · RME, MADI Converter, cap. 7.1 (fabricante; AES10 no leída)
- «MADI, the serial Multichannel Audio Digital Interface, has been defined already in 1989 as an extension of the existing AES3 standard». «MADI contains 28 of those AES/EBU signals in serial [...] and the sample rate can still even vary by +/-12.5%. The limit which cannot be exceeded is a data rate of 100 Mbit/s.»
- «the 64 channel mode was introduced officially in 2001. It allows for a maximum sample rate of 48 kHz + ca. 1%, corresponding to 32 channels at 96 kHz [...]. The effective data rate of the port is 125 Mbit/s due to additional coding.»
- «Older devices understand and generate only the 56 channel format.»
- Coaxial: «Coaxial via BNC, 75 Ohm, according to AES10-1991». Distancia: «distances of up to 100 meters using coaxial cables». Especificación del equipo: «Cable length: more than 100 m». Niveles: entrada «error-free from about 180 mVpp», salida «around 400 mVpp when terminated with 75 Ohm».
- Óptico: «Optical via FDDI duplex SC connector», «62.5/125 and 50/125 compatible». «The cables have an internal fibre of only 50 or 62.5 µm diameter and a coating of 125 µm.» Son de vidrio: «these are always (!) glass fibre cables. Plastic fibre cables (POF [...]) can not be manufactured in such small diameters.» Conector: «SC. Please don't mix them up with ST connectors». Alcance: «The transmission uses the multimode technique which supports cable lengths of up to almost 2 km. Single mode allows for much longer distances, but it uses a completely different fibre (8 µm).» Luz: «(1300 nm)».
- Correcciones a RTVE sonido/17: el monomodo tiene fibra de «8 µm» según RME, no «9 micras» (la cifra de 9 no tiene fuente). Y el 96 kHz da 32 canales («corresponding to 32 channels at 96 kHz»), que es más preciso que decir sólo «reduce el número de canales». La afirmación «el MADI óptico es multimodo» queda confirmada por la fuente de fabricante.

### 11.4 ADAT · RME, ADI-648 (fabricante)

Por tanto hay que fijar el modo a mano.

### 11.5 Audio embebido en SDI · SMPTE 272M-2004 (SD) y ST 299-1:2009 / 299-2:2010 (HD)
- 272M alcance: «the mapping of AES digital audio data, AES auxiliary data, and associated control information into the ancillary data space of serial digital video conforming to ANSI/SMPTE 259M or SMPTE 344M». «Audio sampled at 48 kHz and clock locked (synchronous) to video is the preferred implementation for intrastudio applications.» Por defecto: «20 bits of audio data», con 24 bits como opción. «a minimum of two audio channels and a maximum of 16 audio channels based on available ancillary data space in a given format (four channels maximum for composite digital)». Grupos: «Channels 1 through 4 are in group 1, channels 5 through 8 are in group 2, and so on.»
- 299-1 alcance: «the mapping of 24-bit AES digital audio data and associated control information into the ancillary data space of a serial digital video conforming to SMPTE 292». Admite datos no PCM: «non-PCM data formatted according to SMPTE 337» (así viaja Dolby E).
- 299-1 §1.2: «Audio sampled at a clock frequency of either 48 kHz or 96 kHz locked (synchronous) to video, is the preferred implementation for intrastudio applications.» Opcional de 32 a 48 kHz, síncrono o asíncrono.
- 299-1 §1.3: «Audio channels are transmitted in groups of four, up to a maximum of 16 audio channels in the case of 32 kHz, 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz sampling, and up to a maximum of 8 audio channels in case of 96-kHz sampling. Each group is identified by a unique ancillary data ID.»
- 299-1 §1.4: «Audio data packets are multiplexed (embedded) into the horizontal ancillary data space of the Cb/Cr data stream, and audio control packets are multiplexed into the horizontal ancillary data space of the Y data stream.» (HANC)
- 299-2: «defines an SMPTE 291M Type 1 packet structure for identifying audio channels numbered from 17 to 32, beyond the 16 channels defined in SMPTE ST 299-1». Cada grupo extendido lleva «up to four 24-bit audio channels with 32, 44.1, or 48-kHz sample rates, or up to two 24-bit audio channels with 96-kHz sample rate».

- Paso a IP: la ST 2110-30 da el símbolo «SGRP» («One SDI audio group», 4 canales) y la nota «SDI allows the carriage of at least 16 embedded audio channels» (§15.5).
- Coaxial de vídeo SDI y BNC: la ST 276M da BNC y 75 Ω para AES3. Para el SDI no se ha leído la norma de capa física (SMPTE 292/259 no descargadas).

### 10.1 Ley del cuadrado inverso y distancia crítica · Shure/Frank
- «the inverse square law which states that as listeners double their distance from the sound source, the SPL they perceive will decrease by 6.02dB». Fórmula: «L' = L + 20 log D - 20 log D'». Sólo en «a so-called "free field."»
- Percepción (según el autor): «A one dB increase is barely audible, 3dB is a generally noticeable change, and a 10dB increase is considered to be twice as loud.»
- Distancia crítica: «The term "critical distance" is defined as the distance from a sound source where the direct sound is the same level as the reverberant sound.» «when the listener is within the critical distance of the source, the inverse square law works pretty well. Outside the critical distance things get much more complex.»

### 10.2 Realimentación: NAG, PAG, margen, NOM · Shure/Frank
- NAG: la ganancia que el sistema debe dar para que el oyente más lejano oiga como el más cercano («This is called the needed acoustic gain (NAG)»). Ejemplo: 21 dB.
- Distancias: «D1 is the distance between the microphone and the loudspeaker»; «D0 is the distance between the talker and the farthest listener»; «D2 is the distance between the loudspeaker and the farthest listener»; «DS is the distance between the talker and the microphone».
- «PAG = 20 log D1 - 20 log D2 + 20 log D0 - 20 log DS». Supuesto: «omnidirectional microphones and loudspeakers and it neglects the effects of reverberation and echo (as if the system were outdoors)».
- Qué subir y qué bajar: hay que hacer D1 y D0 lo mayores posible y D2 y DS lo menores posible. DS es «the most important distance to change».
- Margen: «Virtually all systems need to be operated with a safety margin (called the feedback stability margin), usually 6dB, to avoid the annoying ringing sound». Ecuación con margen: «... - 6».
- Micrófonos abiertos: «PAG = 20 log D1 – 20 log D2 + 20 log D0 – 20 log DS – 10 log NOM - 6». «each time the NOW [sic: NOM] doubles, the PAG is decreased by 3dB.» Conclusión: «the number of open microphones needs to be limited as much as possible.»
- Errata aritmética de la fuente: con 2 micrófonos habla de «new 11dB PAG» cuando 24 − 3 = 21 dB (el resultado de «0dB» con NAG 21 sólo cuadra con 21). No copiar el 11.
- Esto sostiene con fuente la lista de RTVE (acercar el micrófono, alejar los altavoces del micrófono) y la regla de 6 dB por doblar la distancia.

### 10.3 Arrays lineales (PA) · Ureda, JAES 2004
- Campo cercano y lejano: la presión de una fuente lineal «undulates near the source while generally decreasing in level at −3 dB per doubling of distance. At a certain distance, referred to as the transition distance, the undulations disappear and the response falls off at −6 dB. The near field is defined as the region between the source and the transition distance. Beyond the transition distance is the far field.» «The transition distance is a function of the line source length and frequency».
- Ejemplo: línea recta de 4 m a 8 kHz: −3 dB por doble distancia «out to approximately 100 m» y, más allá, «−6 dB per doubling of the distance».
- Historia: cita de 1963 a Klepper y Steele: «Line-source loudspeaker arrays, often called ‘column’ loudspeakers».
- Formas: además de rectos, «curved (arc), J, and progressive line arrays».

### 10.4 Amplificadores · Crown FAQ y TI AN-1497
- Factor de amortiguamiento (Crown): «Damping factor is the ratio of the rated speaker impedance to the amplifier's output impedance.» Si las resonancias del altavoz no quedan cortocircuitadas por la etapa, «the speaker output can have an over accentuated or "boomy" bass sound». La baja impedancia de salida se debe a la «negative voltage feedback».
- Cable de altavoz (Crown): «The longer the run, the heavier gauge you will need to minimize power and damping factor loss.» Orientación: «runs of 25 Ft or less work well with 14 gauge. Over 25 one should use 12 gauge if possible» (AWG, costumbre de fabricante).
- Puente mono (Crown): «Place the MODE switch [...] in the Bridge-Mono position. The input connector should be inserted into channel one [...]. Connect the speaker leads across the two red binding posts». Paralelo mono: puente entre las dos salidas rojas y altavoz «across the red and black binding posts». Hay modelos que no lo admiten.
- Sensibilidad de entrada (Crown): «For amplifiers with a 0.775V position, this positions corresponds to a 0 dBu level. The 1.4V position corresponds to a +4 dBu level. The 26 dB position is a fixed gain position.»
- Conector Speakon (Crown): «Speakons allow both channels of amplifier output to be wired to the same connector.» Modelo citado: NL4FC.
- Clase AB y clase D (TI): «Class AB exhibits a theoretical peak efficiency of 78%, and is only 30% - 40% efficient at normal operating levels. Efficiency is where the switching, or Class D, audio amplifier has large advantages over linear classes.» Ejemplo: «a 90% efficient amplifier at 1W output dissipates 100mW». La clase D produce una señal PWM de alta frecuencia: «the class D outputs a high frequency PWM signal». Es una nota de amplificadores para dispositivos portátiles: vale para el concepto, no para las potencias.
- XLR y jack TRS (Crown, también para el tema 11): «XLR Pin 1 is ground (shield). Pin 2 is hot (non-inverting) Pin 3 is low (inverting) 1/4" TRS Tip: hot (non-inverting) Ring: low (inverting) Sleeve: Shield (ground)».
