Titan Tube is the ultimate LED tube for filmmakers, studios, event technicians and any creative person. It emits powerful, tunable whites with ultra-high color rendering as well as colored light which can be applied to individual pixels or the whole tube. The tube offers unlimited range of usage; indoor or outdoor, AC-powered or on battery, on the go with the AsteraApp, with wired or wireless DMX.

Mounting accessories
TubeStand
Tripod with M5 thread to set up your tube vertically. Can be folded for storage. Compatible with Titan Tube, Helios Tube, AX1 PixelTube.
More about TubeStand
TubeHolder
Essential grip to mount tubes. Connect Astera’s BabyPin to the backside 1/4"-20 thread to insert it into Super Clamps and Gobo Heads. Comes with pin to keep it securely locked.
More about TubeHolder
EyeBolt
EyeBolt with M5 thread that works with Astera’s tubes and a wide range of other Astera products. Also used as safety mounting point for tubes.
More about EyeBolt
WingPlate
Mounting plate to attach a tube to a single mounting point. Also used to connect 2 tubes to form a line, triangle, square etc. Front side of WingPlate takes 2 TubeHolders (included with each tube), backside has a 1/4"-20 thread for Astera’s BabyPin.
More about WingPlate
CrossPlate
Up to four WingPlates can be attached in parallel to one CrossPlate to create a 4bank or panel effect. A Baby Pin is welded to the CrossPlate’s backside for a reliable grip.
More about CrossPlate
