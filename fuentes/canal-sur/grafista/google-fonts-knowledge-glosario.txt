X-height – Fonts Knowledge - Google Fonts
The height of the lowercase characters that have no ascenders nor descenders, which indicates how tall or short the type appears.

Ascenders & descenders – Fonts Knowledge - Google Fonts
The upward or downward parts of letterforms that extend above the x-height—and usually above the cap height (ascenders)—and drop below the baseline (descenders).

https://fonts.google.com/knowledge/glossary/baseline
Baseline – Fonts Knowledge - Google Fonts
The invisible line upon which a line of type rests. It’s used as a point from which other elements are measured, such as leading (line height) and x-height.

Legibility – Fonts Knowledge - Google Fonts
Describes how a typeface’s glyphs can be correctly identified as characters and words, including how individual characters can be distinguished from one another.

Readability – Fonts Knowledge - Google Fonts
Describes how comfortable it is for a person to engage with text created with legible type.
