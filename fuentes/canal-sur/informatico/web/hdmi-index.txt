HDMI® Technology

Almost 14 billion devices enabled with HDMI® technology have shipped since the first HDMI specification was released in December 2002. The latest HDMI 2.2 Specification continues to enable the development of new product categories and innovative solutions to meet the growing demand for higher performance and more immersive consumer experiences.

HDMI technology continues as the leading digital video, audio and data interface that connects ultra high-definition displays to a wide range of consumer electronics, PC, mobile, automotive and commercial AV devices. It also has expanded into enabling solutions for industries such as healthcare, military, aerospace, security and surveillance, and industrial automation.

The worldwide ecosystem of HDMI-connected devices and solutions includes a network of Licensed HDMI Adopters, Authorized Test Centers, Authorized Test Equipment Manufacturers, manufacturers, resellers and installers.
