 MediaCentral | Production Management
 Unify production teams to collaborate on media and projects from anywhere.
 MediaCentral delivers production environments that support award-winning content audiences love.
 KEY FEATURES
 Media management
 Take control of your valuable media assets, tracking them from ingest to archive and every step in-between. From single resolution to multi-resolution, it’s your media, manage it your way.
 Collaborative editing
 From journalists working on news packages to researchers putting together roughcuts to craft editors delivering the final edit, scale up to hundreds of users working together to create great shows.
 Detailed permissions
 From no access to having administrative rights, Production Management delivers highly sophisticated permissions management to ensure the right access to the right team at the right time.
 Workflow and process automation
 Drive efficiencies in your organization with the power of integrated media services such as transcoding, archiving, restoring, and more to automate manual processes and deliver enhanced collaboration.
 Content sharing at scale
 Production Management is driven by Avid NEXIS shared storage, the industry-leading solution with unmatched workflow integration ensuring reliable performance every time.
 Metadata management
 Enhanced content discoverability with both system and user generated metadata for ultra-fast searching across connected clients such as Media Composer, Adobe Premiere Pro and the web-based MediaCentral | Cloud UX.
