 MediaCentral | Newsroom Management 
 Create, Collaborate, and Produce for Engaged Audiences
 Watch the video 
 MediaCentral | Newsroom Management is the newsroom computer system (NRCS) you need to break stories on-air faster and captivate your broadcast audience. Unite your team with a modern newsroom solution that connects teams, automates tedious production tasks, and delivers global, award-winning news.
 KEY FEATURES
 Power on-air production
 Let your creativity flow and deliver shows audiences love with the unmatched reliability of MediaCentral | Newsroom Management (iNEWS).
 New story writing experience for journalists working with MediaCentral | Newsroom Management (iNEWS) now include importing text from the Wolftech Story to the script, adding metadata, two ways sync and more. 
 Story writing on the go
 Quickly get to the heart of the story with dedicated tools for creative writing and instant sharing with the rest of the editorial team.
 And now with Wolftech's native mobile app, Wolftech Go, empower users to work efficiently in the field, including collaborating on iNEWS stories. Create and edit iNEWS stories, upload material, contribute to news gathering and story planning or request and get support from your legal, rights or graphics teams all on the go. Discover a modern mobile app interface for a connected newsroom experience in sync with your trusted iNEWS client.
 Find out how 
 Live production capabilities 
 Build rundowns, adjust timing, go live and make real-time changes—even on-air— with the lightning-fast performance of the modernized iNEWS client .
 Control room coordination 
 Streamline your control room operations by integrating your rundown with studio automation, teleprompters, graphics, and video playout servers.
 Read more 
 Text and video together 
 Write, produce and edit directly in a web browser while expanding your newsroom into video production through MediaCentral | Cloud UX . Or create iNEWS stories, upload material, contribute to newsgathering and story planning on the go with the Wolftech GO and Reporter apps .
 Planning to publication 
 Streamline operations and reduce costs from initial ideas and fact-finding to multi-platform publication. Discover how your connected Wolftech News  application allows your teams to plan, assign and break news as they happen directly from location.
