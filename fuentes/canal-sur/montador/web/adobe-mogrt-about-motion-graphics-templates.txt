Work with Photoshop and Premiere
Overview of Motion Graphics templates
Last updated on
Jan 7, 2026
Learn more about Motion Graphics templates that can help you elevate your video projects with dynamic and professional designs.
A Motion Graphics template is a file type (.mogrt) that can be created in Adobe Premiere or Adobe After Effects. Packaged as templates with easy-to-use controls designed to be customized in Premiere, these templates give you the power of After Effects motion graphics.
Use Motion Graphics templates to create various animated elements, such as titles, lower thirds, and buttons. Use the
Graphics Templates
panel to browse
Motion Graphics
and
Adobe Stock
assets.
You can also bring Motion Graphics templates into Premiere from the following locations:
Local Templates Folder
Creative Cloud Libraries
Adobe Stock
Create and export your motion graphics as templates, install them, and add them to your projects whenever required.
