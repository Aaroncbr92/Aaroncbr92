Work with Photoshop and Premiere
Use data-driven Motion Graphics templates
Last updated on
Jan 7, 2026
Learn how to use data-driven Motion Graphics templates to efficiently customize charts and animations with spreadsheet data, creating dynamic visuals for your projects in Adobe Premiere.
Data-driven Motion Graphics templates allow you to quickly and accurately customize bar charts, line graphs, and more, without manual entry or deep knowledge of motion graphics.
You can create these data-driven mogrts in After Effects, or get free or premium mogrts on Adobe Stock. You can access them directly from the
Graphics Templates
panel in Premiere.
Data-driven Motion Graphics templates can contain three types of data:
Text
Color
Numbers
Note
The author sets the data type for each column in After Effects, and the data types cannot be changed in Premiere.
When unmapped, the types translate as a text string for text data, a hot text slider for number data, or a hexadecimal value with a color swatch for color data. Mapped data show as labels, disabled for manual entry.
Open the
Graphics Templates
panel, find, and drag the desired
Motion Graphics
template into a Premiere sequence.
Add numbers to the elements in the template and adjust colors, fonts, and other visual settings as needed.
Fine-tune visual aspects like colors, fonts, and other design settings to suit your project using the Properties panel.
