FAQs
Can Mo-Sys camera tracking be used for green screen virtual production?
Yes, the same StarTracker tracking technology used for LED volumes also supports green screen virtual production. In a greenscreen virtual production setup, camera tracking data tells the compositing software how to move and scale the digital background to match the live camera, so the composite holds together even as the camera pans, tilts, or moves through the space. Mo-Sys tracking works the same way whether the final output is composited live or displayed on an LED wall, giving productions flexibility to choose the workflow that fits their space and budget.
Does Mo-Sys camera tracking work for VFX and visual effects production beyond live broadcast?
Yes, Mo-Sys camera tracking is used well beyond live broadcast, supporting film, commercials, and other VFX-heavy productions. Recorded StarTracker data can be exported with an FBX file for use in Maya and other post-production tools, giving visual effects teams accurate camera and lens data to match CG elements to the plate.
